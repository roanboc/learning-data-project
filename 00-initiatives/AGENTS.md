# AGENTS.md

Analytics' records and the initiatives' documentation, as text. At the university they live in
Analytics' own records and in Jira; here they stand in for both.

- **A question starts here.** A new question gets the next `AQ` number in
  [`analytics-questions.md`](analytics-questions.md) before anything else changes. The architecture
  then records it as a driver of the same number.
- **Business users ask and approve in tickets.** The people who asked approve a requirement and close
  its ticket once they've checked the result; they never review a pull request.
- **An agent may draft** a question's record or a requirement from a conversation, on a branch. A person
  from Analytics reviews it; an agent never approves, accepts a requirement or closes a ticket.
- **The data team may suggest.** From a goal's tree in [`goals/`](goals/learning-and-teaching.md), it offers
  the goal's owner a question nobody has asked, under the next `SQ` number there. Nothing else changes
  until the owner says yes in a ticket; then the question takes the next `AQ` number.
- **Say what's known.** Most questions come with no known answer: write "No", and who checks the result,
  and how.
- **Links point one way.** Nothing here reads the other folders.
